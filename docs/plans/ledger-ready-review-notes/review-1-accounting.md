# Review 1, accounting lens (Codex, 2026-10-07) on ledger design rev 1. Verdict: reject as ledger-ready.

Blockers
1. Explicit tax point: freeze taxPointDate, taxPointReason, taxable amount, refs. Ordinary sale: delivery, modified by invoicing (§23 stk 2). Advance payment: §23 stk 3 at receipt when supply specified. Allocation is never the trigger. Distinguish payment request from qualifying advance VAT invoice. (skat.dk 2060294, 2060295)
2. "Shift the VAT line" unbalances. Receipt-first advance: Dr cash 125 / Cr prepayments 100 / Cr VAT 25. If receipt credits customer credit first: recognition event Dr customer credit 125 / Cr prepayments 100 / Cr VAT 25. prepayments_received excludes VAT already recognized. Unpaid advance demand is not "money received"; separate policy.
3. Deposit conservation: per deposit VAT group track paid, applied, credited, refunded with cumulative limits; partial application leaves liability; cancellation needs deposit-specific correction + refund; crediting a final invoice restores/discharges applications; retained cancellation fees need own VAT classification; foreign-currency deposits need base amounts.
5. Credit note is not a mirror: credit the credited portion with its actual treatment; amount mode must say net or gross; targeted vs whole-document; freeze credited groups, original refs, debtor carrying value; FX treatment specified (not fresh rate); bound cumulative credits; preserve original rounding on full cancellation; emit allocation reversals on paid invoices.
6. Unattributed -> identified needs an event: payment.identified Dr unattributed_receipts / Cr customer_credit. Refund unidentified from its actual role.
7. Refunds/voids need carrying values: EUR 100 liability at 745 refunded for DKK 750 = Dr liability 745 / Dr FX loss 5 / Cr bank 750. payment.voided must carry the allocations it reverses in payload. Distinguish void (never happened) from refund/chargeback (real money).
8. FX carrying value: use current carrying value (after revaluation), not original frozen rate; FX recognized at economic settlement date, not click date; persist foreign-currency quantities and position ids for debtor, liabilities, bank, clearing; define revaluation/adjustment contract; third currency needs both quantities + agreed conversion.
9. Stripe payout/conversion: events need account identities, original-currency quantities, carrying amount discharged, destination valuation, base amounts; EUR balance converted later yields separate FX; same-currency transfer preserves carrying value; prevent double-counting conversion; define fee/conversion order.
12. 3-decimal currencies: Decimal(12,2) already lost the digit; integer minor unit prices cannot represent fractional unit prices; Peppol BR-DEC-20 limits VAT breakdown to 2 decimals. Preserve exact inputs; separate unit-price precision from payable precision; refuse unsupported Peppol exports.
13. Write-off recovery missing: freeze loss-established date, eligible principal, VAT, prior declaration ref, evidence (enforcement failure, insolvency, composition, documented assessment; small-claim concessions); compute from remaining debt excluding untaxed interest/charges; prevent duplicate assessments; recovery restores relieved VAT proportionally; partial write-off leaves balance collectible. (skat.dk 1978090, 1978097)
15. Retention: 5 years from end of the financial year the material concerns, across all transactions/corrections; a 2026 invoice settled 2028 extends; enforce holds. (erhvervsstyrelsen.dk vejledning-bogfoeringsloven)

Should-fix
4. Zero-VAT treatments: reverse charge needs statement + buyer VAT id + legal basis + verification evidence; distinguish domestic exempt, zero-rated, intra-community goods, export goods; Peppol O cannot mix with other categories (BR-O-11) and has no rate (BR-O-05); reject unsupported combos.
10. Processor fees: not universally exempt; store breakdown, supplier, voucher ref, tax classification, base valuation; allow expense / input VAT / purchase reverse charge roles or "tax assessment required".
11. Rounding: per-rate-total half-up matches Peppol BR-CO-17; don't claim it is legally mandated; stable ties, cumulative residues for partial credits; inclusive pricing: derive group net/tax from gross then allocate; bound payable rounding; sign convention (positive credits role, negative debits); keep precise unit prices so qty x price reconciles.
14. Dates: occurrence, posting, tax-point are different; moving a late receipt to first open day does not move its VAT period; late corrections need original-period reference + amendment decision; VAT conversion rates have their own rules (retsinformation 2023/1435), user-entered rates are not compliant VAT conversion.
16. Tombstoning is deletion of the operational profile, not anonymization; retained snapshots/PDFs are identifiable under legal-obligation basis; expire ancillary records (drafts, approvals, emails, events?) by purpose; handle backups. Verify with privacy adviser.
17. Tests must assert ending balances and tax-period amounts, plus: unpaid advance demand vs qualifying advance invoice; receipt before invoice; partial deposit receipt/application/refund; cancellation; paid-invoice credit/refund; unidentified receipt identification; chargeback; third-currency payment; year-end revaluation then settlement; foreign Stripe payout; taxable processor fee; partial write-off then recovery; late-period correction; repeated one-cent credits; non-VAT-registered freelancer; retention extended by later settlement.

Open questions: Q1 keep invoice.issued separate. Q2 proportional within one invoice, final allocation absorbs residue, honour explicit identification, use adjusted carrying value after revaluation. Q3 implement tax point now. Q4 block structured exports needing classified tax data, keep PDFs accessible, never auto-classify. Q5 one Stripe account per org, historical mixed payouts unresolved.
