# Quits OSS v0.16.0

Changes since the published v0.15.0 tag (`56d31bb8`), through `df84a0bd`. This minor release covers 25 merged PRs in the self-hostable Quits core.

## Invoices, credit notes and emails

- Invoice and credit-note emails attach the issued PDF. The attachment is the stored PDF, checked against its stored SHA-256, so retries send identical bytes. If the PDF is missing, does not match or is over 7 MB, the email goes without it and without the "attached" line. The credit-note email states the credit note's own number and issue date next to the corrected invoice (#440).
- Draft invoices and quotes carry a "not valid" banner and a diagonal KLADDE / DRAFT watermark on every page, and a draft invoice is titled "Fakturakladde". Issued documents render byte for byte as before (#423).
- A Danish buyer's CVR prints as "CVR-nr." on invoices, credit notes, emails and public pages when it sits only in the contact's tax ID field (#439).
- Invoice and credit-note numbers stay distinct: a save that would give both the same prefix is refused. Once an organization has numbered documents, onboarding no longer rewinds its counters, and a completed legacy onboarding is final (#442).
- An invoice converted from a quote is due after the customer's or organization's payment terms, not on the quote's expiry date (#420).
- Pick or add the customer from the invoice form. Where the runtime supplies a CVR lookup, typing a company name or CVR number searches the register. Otherwise the picker lists existing customers and links to the new-customer form (#429).
- The credit-note page shows the seller and customer like the invoice page, says "Udsteder…" while issuing, and reloads after a failed answer (#425).
- The invoice page follows the interface language for panels, dates and labels. An invalid `/pay` link uses the invoice's or seller's language before the browser's (#437).
- Copy link confirms with "Kopieret". `/pay` groups the account number. Momsland is hidden for domestic Danish invoices and quotes, and the trial banner's last day reads "slutter i dag" (#441).

## Payments and refunds

- When more has been paid than an invoice charges after credit notes, the invoice shows what is owed back to the customer. Record a refund once it has been paid back, or void it with a reason. A payment cannot be voided while refunds stand (#424, #431).
- Overblik's Udestående card shows what is owed to customers and what was refunded this month, per currency (#431).

## Agreements

- Every agreement amount states whether it is excl. or incl. VAT on the page, the form, the PDF and `/a`. A new agreement starts on the organization's VAT rate (#422).
- Creating draft invoices shows progress, then opens the draft when there is one (#426).
- A failed save in the agreement editor shows its error once (#421).

## Onboarding, signup and Settings

- Onboarding hides VAT fields from non-VAT sellers, offers a Selskabsform select for manual setup, and drops redundant badges and notices from the review screen (#432).
- The stepper shows only real steps, and Betaling only once payments are available. Danish copy calls the seller a "virksomhed" (#433).
- Placeholder emails and phone numbers from the CVR register are no longer prefilled (#428).
- The first-send bank nudge saves Reg.nr. and Kontonr. inline and then sends (#436).
- Invite-only signup states the rule once, says what happened after a refusal, and explains the wait while it checks (#434).
- Settings shows the CVR for every Danish seller and explains when SE-nr. applies (#430).
- Danish phone numbers are stored as E.164 and shown in pairs. The quote email shows the seller's address and CVR (#438).
- A handled mutation failure on the main screens shows once, not again in the global banner (#435).
- Session reads and the app layout make fewer database round trips (#427).

## Tests and contributing

Database tests are named `*.integration.test.*` and run with `vitest.integration.config.ts`. Unit runs blank `DATABASE_URL`, and a worktree no longer borrows the main checkout's database (#443). AGENTS.md asks for less interface copy and no eyebrow labels (#444).

## Upgrading

The workspace, app, contracts and shared package versions move together. Package manifests are the version authority. Apply the new database migration through the normal migration path before serving the updated app:

- `20261022100000_payment_refunds`

It adds the `payment_refund` table. Existing rows are not changed.

- Hosts can set `requireBuyerCvr` in the invoice issuance policy (`resolveInvoiceIssuancePolicy`) to refuse invoices without a Danish buyer CVR or SE number. It is off by default, so self-hosted installs are unaffected (#439).
- Document emails attach the issued PDF when it is stored and at most 7 MB. Otherwise the email is sent without it and `document.email.pdf_not_attached` is logged with the reason. The outbox message schema gains an optional `attachments` field (#440).
- Settings and onboarding saves that would make the invoice and credit-note prefixes equal are refused by `assertDistinctDocumentPrefixes`. Organizations that already have equal prefixes can still save other settings (#442).
- Contributors: tests that need a database must be named `*.integration.test.*`. The unit config sets `DATABASE_URL` to empty, so they fail there (#443).

This release does not configure or deploy any hosted service.
