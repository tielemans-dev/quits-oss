# Quits OSS v0.8.0

Changes since v0.7.0 (`d81e679`), through `df7352e`.

## Settlement receipts and allocations

Record payments received and allocate them to invoices, with reviewed corrections (#72). Includes migration `20261014010000_settlement_receipts`. Discount and write-off actions stay disabled pending accountant review.

## Calendar dates west of UTC

**Calendar dates shown one day early west of UTC (fixed).** In a timezone west of UTC, such as the Americas, due dates and quote expiry dates were one day early in several places. Organisations at or east of UTC, including every Danish one, were not affected. Reminder emails were always correct. Affected, by the release where it started: the invoice PDF, invoice and quote emails and invoice detail page (since v0.1.0); the accounting CSV and the legacy export mapper (v0.2.0); the record frozen at issuance and the snapshot-based UBL (v0.3.0) built from it; and the public pay and quote pages for customers viewing from a western timezone. After upgrading, everything rendered live shows the correct date, for older invoices too: the detail page, public pages, new emails and CSV exports. Due dates that invoices created from deliverables stored with a time of day are normalised to their calendar day. The invoice's `dueDate` is the authoritative due date. Records frozen at issuance are evidence and are not rewritten, so for invoices issued before the upgrade these keep the earlier date: the stored PDF and UBL, `issuanceSnapshot.dueDate` (which agents also see), and the `invoice.issued` event. Issued PDFs are never regenerated. If a customer needs a corrected document, issue a credit note and a new invoice.

The fix (#88) includes migration `20261014000000_normalize_calendar_dates`, which normalises non-midnight invoice due dates and quote expiry dates using each document's stored timezone. UTC-midnight values and rows with blank or unrecognised timezones remain unchanged.
