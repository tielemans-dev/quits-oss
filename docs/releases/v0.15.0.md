# Quits OSS v0.15.0

Changes since the published v0.14.0 tag (`0bb84b20`), through `faa20ba0`. This minor release covers 36 merged PRs in the self-hostable Quits core.

## Sending invoices and agreements

- When Send is refused before the review, the invoice page says so under the button, then scrolls to and focuses the gaps. The gaps link to the company or payment settings that fix them (#393).
- The agreement actions say what they are doing ("Sender…", "Udsteder…") and when the agreement email is still on its way. The page polls until the send settles. Invoice explains why there is nothing to invoice yet: waiting for acceptance, waiting for delivery, or nothing left (#395).
- On Workers, sending an invoice now waits for its email delivery in the request, as quote and agreement sends already do. Each provider call is still capped at 60 seconds. Node keeps its bounded wait (#396).
- A delivery that the host's operation policy still refuses 15 minutes after it was queued now fails, instead of waiting forever. If no provider request was made, the invoice or agreement returns to an editable draft with its last send marked as failed, and an invoice gives its number back when it was the last one allocated. Self-hosted installs without an operation policy are unaffected (#413).

## Overblik and the command palette

- Paste bank statement lines into the palette to record the payments they are. Only lines that match one invoice exactly are ticked; other matches need an invoice picked first. Each payment is recorded on the bank's booking date, capped at today in the organization's timezone. Undo voids what was just recorded (#378, #385).
- While a card is open, Overblik shows what confirming it would do: a ghost row for a new invoice, and the rows a payment would settle or a reminder would reach (#379).
- The sections under the greeting are plain sections between hairlines: Kræver din opmærksomhed, Udestående, På vej ind, Modtaget and Seneste aktivitet. På vej ind leaves out invoices that the attention list already shows. The first-run, getting-started, loading and error states follow the same layout (#388, #389, #390).
- Older payment events in the activity list no longer link to a page that does not exist (#389).
- Add the drawing engine for an ASCII murmuration of starlings. It is not shown on any page yet (#386).

## App style

- Amounts are no longer underlined on app screens. Every status uses one badge with four looks: green for settled, amber for what needs attention, red for failures and a quiet neutral for the rest. Overdue invoices are amber, not red (#382).
- Lists sit on the page between hairlines without a box, with a plain first-run state. "1 fakturaer" is now "1 faktura" (#383).
- Panel headings are sentence case instead of mono caps. Customer initials sit on a neutral circle. Load errors and the 404 page are plain blocks with one action (#384).
- Loading rows reach past the column only on the invoice, quote and credit-note lists (#391).
- Hosts can pin a bar above the app with `--app-inset-top`; the top bar, sidebar and mobile drawer start below it. The menu button now comes before the search field (#398).
- Language, country and currency pickers name options in the interface language and list the organization's own first. Trial bars and quiet status pills are no longer faint (#402).

## Forms, customers and documents

- Line VAT is entered as a percent ("Momssats (%)") and still stored as a fraction. The quote form waits for the organization's currency before it shows amounts (#399).
- The shared date field takes typed dates in the locale's order, such as `20.10.2026`, and keeps the calendar button (#404).
- Customers open on a summary of what is on file, with Edit for the form. Where the runtime supplies a CVR lookup, the new-customer form fills the customer from the CVR register. Self-hosted installs without a lookup show the form as before (#410, #411).
- Phone numbers accept spaces, dots, dashes and a leading `00`, and are stored in compact form. The phone error is localized (#414).
- The agreements list shows number, total and status, and a click anywhere on a row opens the agreement. The terms and deposit-line fields have plain labels and a Markdown hint (#407, #408).
- Agreement lines state their amounts on the offer's price basis, so excl.-VAT offers show net lines that add up to the subtotal (#394).
- Invoice detail pages order the buyer address as the PDF does and name the company once (#401).
- Draft PDFs are named for the document and the customer, such as `kladde-faktura-havbrise-logistik-a-s.pdf`, and say that the payment reference will be the invoice number once sent (#405).
- Unknown app paths show a 404 inside the app shell, and `/customers` paths go to the matching `/contacts` page (#409).

## Language

- People can choose the interface language from the user menu. The choice is kept in a `quits-lang` cookie and applies to server rendering. The order is `?lang`, the saved choice, the organization's locale, the browser's language, then English. Documents, emails and PDFs still use the organization's or the document's locale (#403).
- The public pages `/pay`, `/q`, `/a` and `/reminders/unsubscribe` set `<html lang>` to the document's language. `/q` shows the seller and customer blocks as the quote PDF does (#406).

## Onboarding and Settings

- Hosted organizations with unfinished onboarding see a setup banner instead of a read-only one (#400).
- Onboarding marks required fields, keeps company addresses on two lines, and ends a hung CVR lookup after 10 seconds (#412).
- Firmaprofil shows and edits the CVR number. An unanswered VAT question says so, with nothing preselected. Each save's result shows by the button that made it (#415).
- Settings follows the distribution the server reports, not the one the bundle was built for (#416).
- Settings loads the stored payment terms instead of the 14-day default (#417).

## Tests

End-to-end tests wait for hydration before acting on a page (#392).

## Upgrading

The workspace, app, contracts and shared package versions move together. Package manifests are the version authority. This release adds no database migrations and no required runtime contract changes.

- Runtimes with an operation policy now see refused email deliveries fail after 15 minutes. That tick returns HTTP 500 once, as it does for a rejected delivery (#413).
- Newly saved phone numbers are stored compact, such as `+4512345678`. Stored numbers change only when they are next saved (#414).
- A host that pins a bar above the app should set `--app-inset-top` on `<html>` to its height (#398).

This release does not configure or deploy any hosted service.
